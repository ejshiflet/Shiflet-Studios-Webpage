Meet The Photographer

Hello, my name is Elijah Shiflet, the photographer behind Shiflet Studios. I specialize in portrait photography with a focus on capturing your vision through real moments that feel natural and genuinely you. My approach is relaxed and collaborative, allowing you to feel comfortable in front of the camera.

I hold a master’s degree in forensic chemistry, a background that shaped my attention to detail, patience, and appreciation for telling stories through careful observation. While my academic path trained me to analyze and document with precision, photography allows me to pursue my creative passion.

Whether we’re creating portraits for a personal milestone, professional use, or simply to capture a moment in time, my goal is to deliver images that feel timeless and meaningful. I believe the best photographs come from trust, connection, and thoughtful craftsmanship, and I’m dedicated to creating an experience that is just as enjoyable as the final images.
